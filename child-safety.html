Child Safety Standards

Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Policy

App: Balu
Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

Balu is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Our app allows users to create polls, vote, participate in discussions, and interact with other members of the community.

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse, child sexual exploitation, grooming, or any content or behaviour that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children.

These standards apply to all users and all content and interactions associated with Balu, including polls, comments, replies, profiles, images, videos, and shared links.

2. Prohibited Content and Behaviour

Balu prohibits the following:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including images, videos, and other material depicting or promoting the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.
  • Content that sexualises minors or promotes sexual activity involving children.
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation, or attempts to contact children for sexual purposes.
  • Requesting, sharing, distributing, or promoting material involving child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Sexual exploitation, trafficking, or other activities that put children at risk of abuse.
  • Polls, comments, replies, profiles, links, or other content that encourages, facilitates, or promotes child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Users must not use Balu to create, request, distribute, promote, or facilitate prohibited content or activities.

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report content or behaviour that violates these standards using the reporting features available in the app, where provided, or by contacting us using the contact details below.

Reports should include relevant details, such as the profile, poll, comment, or other content involved and the reason for the report. Do not download or redistribute suspected child sexual abuse material.

We review reports and take appropriate action in accordance with our policies and applicable laws.

4. Enforcement

We take child safety violations seriously. Depending on the circumstances, our response may include:

  • Removing content that violates these standards.
  • Suspending or permanently terminating accounts involved in violations.
  • Restricting access to features to prevent further abuse.
  • Preserving relevant information where appropriate and lawful.
  • Reporting suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation to appropriate authorities or child protection organisations when required or appropriate.

We may take immediate action when content or behaviour presents a serious child safety risk.

5. Cooperation with Authorities

Balu will cooperate with relevant law enforcement agencies and child protection organisations, as required by applicable law, when responding to suspected child sexual abuse and exploitation.
